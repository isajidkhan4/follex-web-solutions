# Follex Web Solutions - Corporate Website

A professional, responsive 6-page website for **Follex Web Solutions**, an eCommerce agency specializing in marketplace management, product listing, and Shopify/WooCommerce development.

## 🚀 Project Overview
This website serves as the digital storefront for Follex, highlighting our expertise in:
- **Marketplace Account Management** (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho)
- **Bulk Catalogue & Product Listing**
- **Shopify & WooCommerce Store Development**

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5** & **CSS3**
- **Bootstrap 5** (Responsive Grid System & Components)
- **JavaScript** (Vanilla JS & AOS for scroll animations)
- **Netlify** (Hosting & Form Handling)

## 📁 File Structure
- `index.html` - Home page (Hero, Stats, Services overview, Portfolio highlights)
- `about.html` - Company story, founder experience, and team profiles
- `services.html` - Deep dive into specific eCommerce services
- `portfolio.html` - Case studies for Paraliya, Prima Medica, and Alnakiyatrading
- `pricing.html` - Engagement models and service tiers
- `contact.html` - Netlify-powered enquiry form and office locations (Jaipur & Bundi)
- `sitemap.xml` & `robots.txt` - SEO configuration
